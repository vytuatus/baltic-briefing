Critical assessment

What the evidence supports, and where the coverage runs ahead of it.

With only one new daily figure since 17 November, the Elevated rating still rests on Latvia's year total, which already exceeds 2025 but counts attempts rather than people; no trend can be read from a few sparse days. The EU process is moving on schedule but behind closed doors: the 20 November forum examined the 2027 pool and possible deductions, and the Baltic states, rated 'at risk', stand to receive priority support rather than new obligations. The only regular movement of migrants into a Baltic state from another EU country remains Estonia's lawful returns to Latvia of people who arrived via Belarus; this is a knock-on effect of Belarus's route, not other EU states shifting their caseload onto the Baltics. How many have been returned is unclear: ERR reported 148 in early November and later implied more than 400, and we corrected ourselves too quickly yesterday. Estonia's road-crossing closure on 1 December is a security measure; a migrant push from Russia afterwards remains a contingency, not an observed movement.

Key reporting

The stories that matter today, each with the type of evidence behind it and a critical take.

EU officials examine the 2027 solidarity pool

Official statement Council agenda document; no press reporting on the outcome found.

The High-Level EU Solidarity Forum met in Brussels on 20 November. Its agenda covered the Commission's proposal for the 2027 solidarity pool and a draft decision letting member states facing a significant migratory situation reduce their contributions. Both documents are classified, and no outcome has been published.

Critical take. This is the step before the Council adopts the pool; last year that came on 8 December. Under the rules states may relocate people, pay or offer other support, so no Baltic state can be made to accept migrants. Whether Estonia or Poland get deductions is the open question.

Estonia's returns to Latvia: two ERR figures that do not match

Official statement Both figures come from police data as reported by ERR; no official breakdown of returns found.

ERR reported on 3 November that 148 of 388 people found in Estonia this year had been handed back to Latvia. In a 16 November report on a smuggling conviction, ERR said over 400 migrants had been caught this year and are then returned to Latvia. In our 21 November edition we concluded that only about 150 had been returned; that was too firm.

Critical take. The difference may be timing or wording, so we now rate the 'over 400 returned' figure as contested rather than wrong. Either way these are lawful returns between neighbours of people who entered the EU via Belarus.

Latvia: 3 attempts on 18 November, about 13,021 this year

Official statement Latvian border guard daily report; counted once.

Latvia's State Border Guard reported stopping 3 people trying to cross from Belarus on 18 November, bringing the year's total to about 13,021. No later daily figures were found. The guard has opened 138 criminal cases over people smuggling this year.

Critical take. Low daily numbers in November follow a much heavier summer. Totals count attempts, and the same people often try repeatedly, so the year total overstates the number of people.

Russia's northern borders: Estonia closes, Finland stays shut

Official statement Estonian decision reported earlier this week; Finnish plan reported by Hromadske in September.

Estonia's five road crossings with Russia close on 1 December, a decision taken over sabotage concerns. Finland's land border with Russia has been closed since December 2023, and its government plans to extend until the end of 2028 the law that allows asylum applications at the border to be restricted in exceptional cases.

Critical take. Both are security measures. Rights groups have criticised Finland's law; no migrant movement toward either border has been reported.
